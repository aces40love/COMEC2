How COMEC helps

Support built around real family needs

COMEC’s work is organized around three simple promises: respond when families need direction, prevent harm through education, and support children and caregivers through difficult circumstances.

1

Respond

Guidance for families dealing with a missing or runaway child, family abduction, or an urgent child-safety concern.

See response services
2

Prevent

Child safety, internet safety, and bullying education designed to help families and community groups recognize risks earlier.

See prevention programs
3

Support

Parent and child counseling support, referrals for behavioral or substance-use concerns, and practical family resources.

See family support
