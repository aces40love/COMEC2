Prevention at home

Small habits can make it easier to respond.

These practices support family communication and readiness without putting responsibility for abuse or exploitation on a child.

1

Keep current information

Maintain a recent photo, accurate height and weight, medical needs, and current contact details. Update the information as children grow.

2

Name trusted adults

Help children identify adults they can contact when they feel unsafe, confused, pressured, or afraid—even if they think they broke a rule.

3

Talk about online contact

Use calm, ongoing conversations about privacy, requests for images, secrecy, unknown contacts, and how to ask for help without fear of punishment.
