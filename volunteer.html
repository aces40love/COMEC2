Volunteer with COMEC

Bring your time, talents, and connections.

Help COMEC reach more neighbors, create welcoming events, and build the relationships that keep free support available to children and families.

Where you can make a difference

Three ways to serve

Choose the role that fits your strengths.

You do not need to be an expert in every part of COMEC’s work. Start with the kind of contribution you enjoy making, and COMEC will help identify a useful next step.

01People & outreach

Community engagement

Be a warm, informed presence for COMEC across the Memphis community.

You might help with

  • Staffing outreach tables and sharing approved resources
  • Representing COMEC at neighborhood, civic, faith, or community gatherings
  • Finding opportunities for presentations and mission awareness
A good fit forConnectors, listeners, ambassadors, and confident communicators
02Hands-on support

Events

Help every COMEC gathering feel organized, welcoming, and mission-focused.

You might help with

  • Guest welcome, registration, and check-in
  • Setup, wayfinding, activity support, and cleanup
  • Golf-tournament, gala, or silent-auction logistics
A good fit forFriendly problem-solvers, team players, and detail-minded helpers
03Skills & connections

Business development

Help turn professional relationships into sustainable community support.

You might help with

  • Introducing COMEC to companies and community partners
  • Identifying sponsorship and in-kind support opportunities
  • Supporting partner outreach, follow-up, and stewardship
A good fit forRelationship-builders, business leaders, sales professionals, and strategists
How to get started

A simple first step.

1

Choose your area

Start with community engagement, events, or business development.

2

Call COMEC

Tell the office what interests you, what skills you bring, and what your availability looks like.

3

Find the right assignment

COMEC will discuss current needs, timing, and any orientation or screening required for the role.

Before you serve: Opportunities vary by current need and schedule. COMEC will explain the responsibilities, supervision, and any screening or training required before an assignment begins.

Make it fit

There is more than one way to contribute.

Tell COMEC what you can offer. A focused contribution can be just as valuable as an ongoing commitment.

Event-basedPitch in around a golf tournament, gala, outreach day, or other scheduled gathering.
OngoingSupport recurring outreach, awareness, follow-up, or relationship-building as needs develop.
Skills-basedPut professional experience, business relationships, or a specialized strength behind the mission.
